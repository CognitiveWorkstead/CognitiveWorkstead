A USEFUL PARTNER, DAY TO DAY

From a tangled thought
to a useful next step.

She is designed to adjust her approach to the work: quick when the answer is simple, more careful when the stakes call for it.

01

Think it through.

Turn a rough idea into options, surface assumptions, compare tradeoffs, and prepare a decision you can explain.

“Help me compare these choices—and tell me what I’m overlooking.”
02

Make the work clearer.

Shape a plan, draft an email, organize notes, prepare for a meeting, or turn a long discussion into concrete next steps.

“Turn this into a practical plan I can start today.”
03

Learn your way.

Explain unfamiliar ideas, break down a problem, adapt the level of detail, and help you practice without pretending that a guess is a fact.

“Explain it using something I already understand.”

Carry useful context.

Keep useful project facts, decisions, sources, and correction history in working records. Use those records to resume the work when they are available to your host.

“Record this decision, its reason, and what it replaces.”
05

Find the right approach.

Consider the capabilities, privacy, and cost a task calls for. Suggest another model or workflow when your current host is a poor fit.

“What does this task need, and can this host actually do it?”
06

Keep your judgment central.

Challenge weak assumptions, distinguish evidence from uncertainty, respect your boundaries, and seek approval when the action calls for it.

“Give me your honest assessment, even if you disagree.”

Is this an app, a model, or a prompt?

It is a structured AI operating profile: a substantial primary Markdown instruction file, plus supporting guides and templates. Your chosen AI host interprets the instructions and supplies the model and tools. The customer ZIP contains no executable software or server.

Which AI platforms can I use?

The package includes guides for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Perplexity, and compatible local models. These are setup paths, not certifications. Free/Plus/Pro browser configurations and the other platform guides remain untested with COL-B. A narrow manual continuity pilot in Codex CLI passed; it does not establish automatic file maintenance or universal compatibility. Read the observed results. File support, context limits, instruction following, memory, and plan features vary.

What do I do with the ZIP?

Extract it and open START-HERE.md. Start a new AI chat, attach COL-B-CORE.md, then send the included activation message. That is the only required import. Say “Start now; use the defaults” if you want to work immediately. Keep using that chat; for a new chat, bring the core and your saved Memory Passport. Nothing is installed.

Can I use a free account? What do Plus and Pro add?

The complete-text/manual-save route is the starting point on a free account, subject to that host’s capacity. Plus and Pro provide host-specific models, tools and usage—not extra COL-B permissions or a guaranteed memory service. An upload is not proof that the original file can be updated. Our account and storage guide separates documented features from actual COL-B test results.

Will she remember everything across chats?

No. Hosts have different memory and storage limits. The Memory Passport gives you a visible way to preserve useful context and carry it forward. You may need to save and reattach it. COL-B should report when a save is unverified or a prior file is unavailable.

Does the profile make my AI more accurate?

It asks the AI to verify meaningful claims, distinguish facts from inference, and acknowledge uncertainty. It cannot guarantee accuracy, force access to sources, or replace qualified professional judgment in high-stakes decisions.

Can I change her name or personality?

Yes. Choose a nickname, tone, amount of humor, and preferred level of detail. COL-B remains the underlying identity. Significant lasting changes should be confirmed; truthfulness and boundaries remain part of the protected behavioral core.

What stays private? Is the core encrypted?

The Markdown profile does not send your chats or passport to Cognitive Workstead. Your AI host’s own privacy and retention rules still apply to what you upload or paste. Sharing material for support is voluntary; do not send credentials or your full passport.

The core is readable text. “Protected core” refers to behavioral rules and available host controls, not unbreakable prompt secrecy or DRM. Personal backups can be encrypted using tools you choose separately.
