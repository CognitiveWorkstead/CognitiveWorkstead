YOUR RIG, WITH CONTEXT

Keep the details.
Find your next step.

A vehicle workspace and an ongoing conversation, with useful tools close by.

01 / BUILD YOUR GARAGE

Begin with the vehicle
you actually own.

Open a vehicle card to see its saved details. Keep equipment, documents, maintenance and service-provider information with that vehicle.

RIG distinguishes saved information from connected readings. A saved mileage entry is a record—not a live odometer.

Start your Garage ↗

02 / WORK THROUGH A QUESTION

Meet COL-B “Colby.”

The AI assistant inside RIG. Open COL-B Chat to work through a question with your saved context and return to the conversation later. Type your question or use the voice control; voice availability depends on your browser, permissions and speech services.

  1. 01

    Describe what you notice.

    Start with one symptom or question and the vehicle it concerns.

  2. 02

    Add what you know.

    Bring observations, relevant records, a photo or a manual. Leave missing information unknown.

  3. 03

    Return with the result.

    Distinguish a suggested check from one you performed, and report what actually happened.

  4. 04

    Keep the useful context.

    Return to the saved conversation and prepare a clearer explanation for qualified help.

You stay in control. AI can be wrong. Colby’s guidance does not establish a proven diagnosis or replace service instructions and qualified help.

03 / KEEP THE HISTORY

The next question
deserves the last record.

Keep maintenance tasks, repair history and upgrade plans with your vehicle. Store the manuals and files you refer to. Return to the details instead of reconstructing them from memory.

Useful things to keep

  • What was done, and when
  • Mileage or hours when known
  • The equipment a record refers to
  • The document behind the information
  • What is still uncertain or unfinished

Record only work that actually happened. A conversation is not proof that a repair was completed.

04 / PREPARE AND RETURN

Your routine.
A little more repeatable.

Bring a checklist you already use, or ask Colby for a draft to review. Keep a reusable master for departure, arrival or seasonal care, work through a checklist, and return to its completed history.

Saved Places is a personal notebook for addresses, coordinates and original notes. It is separate from route planning or turn-by-turn navigation.
