WHAT IT ADDS

A useful view of what’s reported.

↗

Diagnostics with context

See supported speed, RPM, coolant temperature, fuel level, voltage and other reported values. Observation times distinguish current updates from older data; unavailable values remain unknown.

◷

Saved drives

Keep observed vehicle information with its time and vehicle context. Historical readings remain distinct from fresh readings.

⌁

Travel tools together

Trip meters, fuel stops and reusable travel checklists stay with the vehicle. Companion tablet changes can work offline and synchronize after reconnection.

COUNTERS THAT FIT YOUR TRAVEL

A meter for the miles
that matter to you.

Keep multiple named trip meters running at once. Track a vacation alongside the miles since a fuel stop. Reset one meter without stopping the others; its earlier history stays available.

Companion’s meters continue across drive sessions. Distances are GPS estimates; gaps are identified rather than filled with invented mileage.

Made for real travel

  • Add and rename trip meters
  • Reset one while others keep running
  • Preserve earlier meter history
  • Keep fuel stops with the vehicle
  • Synchronize tablet changes after reconnection

Fuel economy needs known fuel quantities and reliable distance. Website edits for an enrolled Companion wait for the tablet’s confirmation.

THE APP COMES FIRST

Start with RIG.
Connect when it fits.

Explore your Garage, records, conversations, checklists and saved places before deciding whether connected hardware fits your needs.

We’re testing rugged tablets and supported adapters. RIG Companion is not a promise that every vehicle, sensor or third-party RV control system can connect.

Before buying hardware

  • Confirm your exact vehicle and adapter support
  • Confirm the device and software build
  • Plan power, secure mounting and connectivity
  • Review what data is collected and shared
