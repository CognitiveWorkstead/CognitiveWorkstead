12 answers

Where do I start?

Open RIG, use its account screen to sign in or create an account, and review access and membership there. Add your first vehicle, keep one useful record and ask Colby a question. Marketing pages do not create an account or take payment.

Who is COL-B “Colby”?

Colby is the AI assistant inside RIG. Ask about your vehicles, saved information or a question you are working through. It can be wrong; confirm important facts, follow the service instructions and use qualified help.

Do I need a Companion device to begin?

The app provides your Garage, saved records, conversations, checklists and places. Connected readings require an appropriate Companion setup. Review compatibility before buying hardware.

Can I use my own checklist?

The v3 Checklists workspace provides import/paste, reusable master templates, working checklists and completed history. Review any Colby-generated draft before using it.

Is Places a navigation or trip-planning app?

No. Places is a personal notebook of addresses, coordinates and original notes. It does not provide route planning or replace a navigation service.

Why are dashboard readings missing or old?

A device, adapter, vehicle or network may not be reporting. Check the status and observation time. A missing reading is unknown, not zero. Recorded information from an earlier drive is historical.

Will every vehicle or RV system work?

No universal compatibility is promised. Support depends on the exact vehicle, adapter, device, software and available sensors. Third-party RV automation and control systems require separate validation.

How do credits and membership work?

The Pricing page explains the displayed monthly and annual prices and Boost. The app shows the offer and allowances for your account before purchase. Promotional balances do not establish standard plan allowances.

How do I ask about being a Founding Tester?

Use the optional tester inquiry on Get started. Tell us your setup and one thing you want RIG to help with. An inquiry is not automatic enrollment and does not promise free service, hardware or a release date.

Does RIG replace a technician?

No. RIG helps organize information and guide questions. AI guidance may be incomplete or wrong. Follow manufacturer instructions and use qualified help for safety checks, service and repairs. Do not interact with the screen while driving.

What should I know about connected data and voice?

Vehicle and GPS information can be sensitive. Review the active app permissions and settings before connecting or sharing. Microphone and speech services may process audio online. Share only what is needed, and remove VINs, precise locations and private account information from public screenshots.

Who makes RIG?

RIG is a Cognitive Workstead product, informed by founder Greg Cothran’s RV ownership experience. Cognitive Workstead builds practical AI systems with human oversight. The company-home link is at the top of every RIG page.

Safety comes with the context.

Use the app while parked. Do not clear codes, change settings, disconnect systems or perform a repair merely because AI suggests it. The selected software workflow and vehicle support must be verified separately from an adapter’s capabilities.

Share thoughtfully.

Keep account, vehicle and location details private when they aren’t needed. Review the app’s current account and data controls. This summary does not replace the terms or privacy information presented by the service you use.
