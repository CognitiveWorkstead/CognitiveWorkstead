RIG CARAVAN · IN DEVELOPMENT

Your people. Your rigs.
One Caravan.

A simpler way to stay connected when you travel together.

We’re building reusable travel groups, trip-by-trip location sharing and clear check-ins into the RIG experience. Caravan is in development; public access and a launch date have not been announced.

A GROUP YOU CAN TRAVEL WITH AGAIN

Keep the people.
Start a fresh trip.

The planned experience lets RIG subscribers create a password-protected Caravan and keep its membership between trips. Start a separate trip when your group heads out again.

Invite riders, assign a leader and a sweep at the back of the group, and keep coordination with the people taking part.

Caravan is designed for phone-only members as well as supported Companion setups. A tablet or MX+ adapter is not intended to be a requirement for basic participation.

YOU CHOOSE WHAT TO SHARE

Join the trip.
Choose your location sharing.

Caravan membership and live-location permission are separate. The design calls for fresh, explicit consent for each trip and vehicle, with the ability to participate in communications without sharing your location.

One live-location trip at a time. Revoking consent or ending the trip stops live sharing. Your vehicle’s private OBD readings are not automatically shared with the group.

Built around clear boundaries

  • Private, authenticated Caravan access
  • Per-trip location choices
  • Visible age and connection status
  • Minimal retained location history
  • Role and membership access checks

These are product requirements. Final device behavior, retention details and release readiness are still being qualified.

KEEP YOUR FAVORITE NAVIGATION

Coordinate the group.
Use the navigation you know.

A leader will be able to share an external trip-plan link. Open it using your preferred browser or navigation app, while Caravan handles the group’s updates and check-ins.

Separation and loss-of-contact notices are planned to help leaders spot a possible problem. They depend on available location and connectivity information.

Before the first public trip

Live sharing, maps, phone background behavior and Companion integration need final validation. Network coverage and device settings affect updates.

Check-ins do not contact emergency services automatically or guarantee that another member has seen a message.

MEMBERSHIP & AVAILABILITY

Part of the RIG direction.
Still being built.

The product plan includes Standard Caravan participation for active RIG subscribers, with an optional individual Enhanced upgrade. Enhanced pricing and availability have not been announced. No Caravan charge or upgrade is available from this page.
