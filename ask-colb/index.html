Where should my organization start with AI?

Start with a workflow people already understand: reporting, intake, review, documentation, support, scheduling, knowledge retrieval, or decision preparation. The best first AI project is narrow, observable, and useful even before it becomes automated.

What should not be automated?

Do not automate judgment-heavy decisions, regulated determinations, security-sensitive access, private data handling, or customer commitments without strong review. AI can help prepare information, but humans should keep control where context, accountability, and risk matter.

What is a practical first AI pilot?

A practical pilot has one workflow, one user group, one success measure, and one review loop. Examples include summarizing field notes, drafting standard reports, searching internal knowledge, triaging requests, or preparing meeting briefs.

What are Colby Systems?

Colby Systems are specialized AI work assistants shaped around real tasks: architecture, executive advising, field reporting, retirement planning, operational awareness, and structured analysis.

How can Cognitive Workstead help?

Cognitive Workstead helps map the work, choose a responsible AI use case, design the pilot, set review boundaries, and turn the result into a working operating pattern.
